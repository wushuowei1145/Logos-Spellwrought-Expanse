Instrument samples used by Logos: The Alchemical Crucible (logos_dimension)
本模组乐器使用的第三方录音采样

The files under assets/logos_dimension/sounds/instrument/ (except celesta/, which is synthesized by this mod)
are derived from the sample libraries below. They are not covered by this mod's "All Rights Reserved" license.
assets/logos_dimension/sounds/instrument/ 下的文件（celesta/ 除外，钢片琴是本模组自己合成的）取自下列采样库，
不适用本模组的「保留所有权利」协议。

Changes / 修改:
  Mixed down to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, silence before the attack trimmed, decaying notes cut and faded
  out, loop points and loop crossfades added to sustained notes, levels adjusted, re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis.
  Only some notes and velocity layers are used. See docs/INSTRUMENT_SAMPLES.md and tools/instrument_sources.py
  in the source repository.
  混成单声道、重采样到 44.1 kHz、切掉起音前的静音、衰减的音截短并淡出、持续音加了循环点与交叉淡化、调整了音量，
  重新编码为 Ogg Vorbis；只用了一部分音与力度层。做法见源码仓库的 docs/INSTRUMENT_SAMPLES.md 与 tools/instrument_sources.py。
